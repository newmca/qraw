- **Ported code.** Qraw contains no JavaScript and compiles no draw.io source file, but a number of its C++ source files are ports (translations, with changes) of functions of draw.io (`jgraph/drawio`) and of its mxGraph library. Each says so in its header, in the form `Port of <upstream path> (draw.io, Apache-2.0), modified.` (`git grep "Port of" src`). Other source files only cite the upstream functions whose behaviour they follow.
- **Data derived from draw.io.** The items below were taken from `jgraph/drawio` commit `c72c9a98` (see each file's provenance notes).

**draw.io Desktop** (`jgraph/drawio-desktop`, Apache-2.0) is used as a **reference tool** by `tools/fidelity/`: it is downloaded from the official GitHub release into a directory outside the repository and run headless to export PNGs for comparison. Nothing of it is committed, built into or redistributed with Qraw.

**Field-of-use restriction.** The `LICENSE` files of draw.io's `stencils/`, `shapes/` and `img/` directories add one restriction on top of Apache-2.0: these assets and their derivatives, including format conversions, may not be used in Atlassian products or on the Atlassian Marketplace without permission. Qraw is not such a product. Every vendored copy of such data must carry those LICENSE files unchanged.

## Not vendored

- Vendor icon sets (AWS, Azure, GCP, Cisco, brand logos) and draw.io's `img/` directory have their own usage terms and are **not** bundled. Palette entries that refer to them draw with a placeholder. If they are added later they will ship as separable icon packs, after a review of their terms (see `docs/research/drawio-rendering-data.md` section 8).
- `js/libavoid-js` (draw.io's connector router) is LGPL-2.1 and is not used.
